What I Believe

At my heart, I'm somebody who loves God, and who believes a person finds exactly what they go looking for — every day.

Most of us spend our days looking for what's wrong. I'm guilty of it too. But it takes the same amount of energy to look for what's good as it does to look for what's wrong. One gives life to your soul. The other brings death to your potential.

If you're looking for a reason to be angry at the world, you'll find it.

If you're looking for evidence that I'm a screwup, you'll find it.

If you're looking for evidence that I don't know what I'm doing — that I'm just some guy trying to make sense of a life I don't fully understand — you'll find that too.

But if you look for the good in me, you'll find that as well.

It depends entirely on what you're looking for. So that's my core motivation with people: concentrate on what's good in someone instead of keeping score of what's not. I'll let God decide what's not right. I'll focus on what is.

I think that's also what it means to be a good employer and a good partner — searching for the good in someone is how you help them grow into what they're actually capable of. If all we do is study how dark it is, we forget what the sun feels like. Forge Mode's philosophy is simple: deal honestly with what's wrong, but concentrate on what's right.

Where I Started

I'm just like everybody else — a regular guy. If there's a difference, it's that I recognized the opportunity AI put in front of all of us, and I'm doing everything I can to make the most of it in the small window it's open.

I started this company with a few ideas and a stubborn skull that wouldn't let me quit. Trial and error, trial and error, trial and error — mostly error, if I'm honest. But there were wins in there too, and I believe what came out of it is real.

I could focus on the fact that there are a million web design companies out there, and everybody knows how to use AI, or will soon. I could focus on the fact that I haven't always made the best decisions in business, and that procrastination has been a real problem for me. All of that is true. None of it is the sum of who I am.

Ten years ago, I would've told you I was sliding toward less. I think I'm climbing the other way now. I'm hoping you'll climb with me.

Because I believe if we do this together, Forge Mode doesn't stay small.

The Heart and the Soul

Right now, if I'm honest, Forge Mode is still becoming what it's going to be. I'm not going to pretend I have that fully figured out. But I know what it's built on.

THE HEART Forge Mode — the work, the tools, the business, what pays the bills and builds the thing.
THE SOUL Misfit Ministries, Antisocial, and the outreach behind them — the desire to help fix what's broken, in other people and in myself.

One doesn't work without the other. The heart keeps it alive. The soul is why it's worth keeping alive.

So that's who you'd be partnering with. Not a finished company with all the answers — a person who's climbing, who's decided to concentrate on what's good, and who's asking you to climb with him.

— D, Forge Mode
